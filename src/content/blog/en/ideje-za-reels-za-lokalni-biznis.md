For a local business, what works best is **short videos of 10 to 30 seconds that show a process, a result or the people behind the work**: how a coffee is made, before and after a haircut, an order being packed, the morning before opening. You shoot them on your phone, vertically, in daylight. Below are 20 ideas by type of business, and the rules that decide whether anyone keeps watching after the first second.

:::ukratko
- The first second decides everything: movement, a result or a question right at the start.
- **Ten to thirty seconds** is enough for most small business ideas.
- Shoot vertically (9:16), in daylight, with a steady hand or a tripod.
- On-screen text is a must, because many people watch with the sound off.
- The same video can go on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with a caption adapted to each.
:::

## Why Reels, when you already have photos?

Reels are also shown to people who don't follow you. A photo in the feed mostly reaches your followers, while a short video has a chance of being seen by someone in your neighbourhood who has never heard of you. For a local business, it's the cheapest way to let new people know you exist.

The second reason is trust. Video shows things a photo can't: the sound of coffee being ground, the way a hairdresser moves, what the place looks like as you walk in. After that, a customer arrives feeling as if they've already been.

## The rules of a good video, in four points

!slika:ideje-za-reels-za-lokalni-biznis-info

**The first second needs movement or a result.** Don't open with a logo, a greeting or an empty shot. Start with the most interesting moment: the finished cake, the hair after the blow-dry, a full shop window.

The middle shows the process, quickly. Short shots of one to two seconds hold attention better than one long shot.

The end says what to do next: the address, “book by message”, “see you on Saturday”. Put it in on-screen text, not just in your voice.

**On-screen text carries the message even without sound.** Large letters, short sentences, kept away from the edges, where the app's buttons won't cover them.

## How long should a Reel be?

Instagram allows longer videos too, but according to the available data, videos longer than three minutes aren't recommended to people who don't follow you. For a local business that's not a problem anyway: **most ideas need less than 30 seconds**. People are more likely to watch a shorter video to the end, and even several times, and that's a sign the content is good.

The rule is that a video lasts as long as the thing you're showing, sped up. Three minutes of making a coffee becomes 15 seconds.

## Sound and music

Business accounts on Instagram don't have access to all the music in the library, because some songs are licensed for personal accounts only. For a business account, the safe options are original sounds (the grinder, the oven, the scissors), your own voice and library music marked as available for businesses. A popular song from the internet, added to the video outside the app, can get the sound or the whole video removed.

Original sound has another advantage: nobody else has it. The crackle of a bread crust becomes your signature, and after a few videos people start to recognise it.

## Shooting on your phone: five tips

- Wipe the lens. It sounds silly, but half of all blurry videos are down to a finger on the glass.
- Shoot by a window, during the day. Daylight beats any lamp.
- Put your phone on a tripod or lean it against something. A steady picture looks professional.
- Shoot more short clips than you need, then pick the best ones.
- Shoot a whole week's material in one go, say on Monday morning.

Shooting in batches saves the most time. Once you've set up the tripod and the light, **five videos take as long as one**. Put them straight into your [content calendar](@plan-objava-za-drustvene-mreze), with a day and a topic, and during the week all you have to do is write the captions.

## One video, four networks

The same vertical video can go on Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. What changes is the caption: shorter and more relaxed on TikTok, a title of up to 100 characters with the #Shorts tag on YouTube, and a caption with a call to action and up to five hashtags on Instagram. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long should a Reel be for a small business?

Between 10 and 30 seconds for most ideas. According to the available data, videos longer than three minutes aren't recommended to people who don't follow you.

### Do I need professional equipment?

No. A phone, daylight and a tripod costing a few thousand dinars (RSD) are enough. The idea and the first second matter more than the camera.

### Can I use popular music?

On a business account, use only the music the app offers you as available for businesses, or original sound. A song added outside the app can get the sound or the video removed.

### Should I put the same video on TikTok too?

Yes, with an adapted caption and without another app's watermark. The networks don't like videos with a competitor's logo in the corner.
