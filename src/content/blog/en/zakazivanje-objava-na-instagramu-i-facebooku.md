You can schedule Instagram and Facebook posts in three ways: **in the Instagram app itself, in the free Meta Business Suite and in a tool that publishes to several networks**. For one profile and the occasional post, Meta's own tools are enough. When you run several networks or several brands, or a client approves the posts, a multi-network tool saves the most time.

:::ukratko
- Scheduling lets you make a post when you have some peace and quiet, and it goes out when your audience is online.
- **Meta Business Suite is free** and schedules for Facebook and Instagram together.
- For LinkedIn, TikTok, Google Business Profile and the other networks, you need another tool or manual work.
- Check the time zone before you schedule anything for the first time; it's the most common mistake.
- Scheduled posts made through the official tools have no known reach penalty.
:::

## Why schedule posts?

The best time to post is often exactly when you're busiest. A café is full at 8 in the morning, a salon at 6 in the evening, and that's when nobody has time to write a caption. Scheduling splits the work into two jobs: making the post, when you have some peace, and publishing it, when your audience is on their phones. When that is for your profile is covered in the article on [the best time to post](@najbolje-vreme-za-objavu-na-instagramu).

The second reason is **rhythm**. When you schedule the whole week's posts on Monday morning, they go out even if you fall ill, go to a trade fair or simply forget. Regularity is half the battle on social media.

## Method 1: in the Instagram app itself

Professional accounts on Instagram (business and creator) can schedule a post, a carousel or a Reel directly from the app:

1. Create the post as usual, up to the caption screen.
2. Open the extra settings (More options or Advanced settings).
3. Turn on Schedule this post and choose the date and time.
4. Go back and confirm by tapping Schedule.

You'll find your scheduled posts under Scheduled content on your profile, and that's where you edit or delete them. This is the quickest way to handle a single post, but it's awkward for a plan of twenty posts, because everything happens on your phone.

## Method 2: Meta Business Suite

Meta Business Suite is Meta's free tool for Facebook Pages and the Instagram accounts linked to them. It works in a browser and on your phone. Here's how it goes:

!slika:zakazivanje-objava-na-instagramu-i-facebooku-info

1. Open Meta Business Suite and choose Create post.
2. Select where it goes: your Facebook Page, your Instagram account or both.
3. Add photos or a video and write the text. You can tailor the text separately for each network.
4. Instead of Publish, choose Schedule, then pick the date and time.
5. Confirm. The post appears in the Planner, where you can see the whole week and the whole month.

Meta has changed how far ahead you can schedule over the years, and with Meta's paid Meta One subscription the limit is longer than without it. For a monthly plan this isn't a problem. If you schedule a long way ahead, check in the date picker how far it lets you go.

The Planner in Meta Business Suite is good for one business with Facebook and Instagram. **Its limitation is that it only covers Meta's networks.** LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Google Business Profile are left out.

## Method 3: a multi-network tool

When you post on three or more networks, or run social media for clients, a multi-network tool starts to pay for itself. Its advantages over Meta's Planner:

- one calendar for every network,
- text and image format adapted to each network,
- approval: a client or your manager sees the post and approves it before it goes out,
- several brands in one account, with roles for your team,
- statistics for all your networks in one place.

| Method | Networks | Advantage | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram app | Instagram only | quickest for a single post | everything on your phone, no plan |
| Meta Business Suite | Facebook and Instagram | free, weekly planner | Meta's networks only |
| Multi-network tool | every network the tool supports | one calendar, approval, teams | subscription |

## How does scheduling work in Sova?

Sova schedules and publishes to seven networks through each network's official connection: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest and Google Business Profile. You make a post once, and Sova adapts it to each network. It then goes into the Calendar, where you see it by day and by week.

The time is set in the **brand's time zone**, so a post goes out at 8:00 Belgrade time even when you're working while travelling or your laptop is set to a different zone. If a post doesn't go through the first time, for example because the network isn't responding, Sova tries again and lets you know if it still doesn't work. A client can get an approval link and leave a comment without having an account in the app. How agencies run several clients is covered in the article on [managing social media for multiple clients](@vodjenje-drustvenih-mreza-za-vise-klijenata).

When you switch on Autopilot, Sova prepares weekly drafts from your strategy by itself, within the budget and rules you've set. On TikTok, LinkedIn and Pinterest it doesn't publish entirely on its own but reminds you to confirm. You can see the flow from plan to published post on the [home page](/#how).

## The most common scheduling mistakes

- Wrong time zone. A post scheduled for 8:00 goes out at 2:00 because the account is set to a different zone. Check before you schedule anything for the first time.
- Schedule and forget. If something changes (a price, your opening hours or the situation in town), the scheduled post goes out as if nothing had happened. Look through your scheduled posts once a week.
- The same text on every network. Scheduling doesn't mean copying. Your LinkedIn text shouldn't be the same as your Instagram one.
- Scheduled, but nobody replies. Comments come in during the first hour after a post goes out. **Someone needs to be there to reply.**

## What can't be scheduled?

The networks allow some features only in the app, live. A live stream can't be scheduled like a regular post, and some options for Reels, such as certain music from the library, are sometimes unavailable through scheduling tools. Stories can be scheduled in Meta's tools and in some other tools, but interactive stickers aren't always supported. When a particular feature really matters to you, make that post in the app.

For everything else the rule is simple: the more posts you schedule in advance, the more time you have for the one thing that can't be scheduled, which is talking to your customers. If you're only just putting a schedule together, start with the articles on [the content calendar](@plan-objava-za-drustvene-mreze) and [how often to post](@koliko-cesto-objavljivati-na-drustvenim-mrezama).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do scheduled posts get less reach?

There's no reliable evidence that Instagram or Facebook penalise posts scheduled through the official tools. Reach depends on the post itself and on the reactions it gets in the first few hours.

### Can I schedule Reels?

Yes, from the Instagram app, from Meta Business Suite and from many multi-network tools. Some options, such as certain music from the library, are sometimes unavailable when you schedule.

### How far ahead can I schedule a post?

It depends on the tool. Meta has changed the limit over the years, so check in the date picker. For a one-month plan, the limits are usually not a problem.

### Is scheduling free?

In the Instagram app and in Meta Business Suite, yes. Multi-network tools usually charge a monthly fee, and many of them, Sova included, offer a [free trial](/register).
