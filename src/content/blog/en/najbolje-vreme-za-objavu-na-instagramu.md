The best time to post on Instagram is **the moment your audience is on their phones**, and for most local businesses in Serbia that means the morning before work (7:00 to 9:00), the lunch break (12:00 to 14:00) and the evening (19:00 to 21:00). Those slots are only a starting assumption. The real answer comes from your profile's insights and a simple four-week test.

:::ukratko
- There is no universal best time, only good starting assumptions.
- **Profile insights** show the days and hours when your followers are active.
- The test takes four weeks: the same type of post in two different slots, then a comparison of reach.
- Timing matters in the first hours after posting, but a weak post doesn't become good because of a good slot.
- Scheduling solves the problem when the best slot falls during your working hours.
:::

## Why does posting time matter at all?

Instagram doesn't show posts strictly in order, but the first hours after posting matter. If a post gets reactions straight away from the people who see it, it gets shown to a wider audience more often. If you post at 3 in the morning, when your followers are asleep, the first reactions only arrive hours later, and by then there are already a hundred newer posts ahead of it.

So timing doesn't create success, but it can slow it down. A good post in a bad slot does worse than it could. A weak post in a perfect slot stays weak.

## Good slots to start with

If you're just setting up your profile, or you don't have enough followers for insights yet, start from your audience's habits. These are starting assumptions for typical local businesses in Serbia, in Belgrade time:

| Type of business | Slots to try | Why then |
|---|---|---|
| Café, bakery, breakfast | 7:00 to 8:30 | people are choosing where to go for coffee or breakfast |
| Restaurant, food delivery | 11:30 to 12:30 and 18:00 to 19:30 | before lunch and before dinner |
| Salon, gym, studio | 19:00 to 21:00 | after work, when people plan their week |
| Clothes shop, gift shop | 20:00 to 22:00 | relaxed scrolling in the evening |
| Services for companies | 8:00 to 10:00 on weekdays | the start of the working day |

The weekend is a story of its own. On Saturday mornings the audience is usually slower and more relaxed, and on Sunday evenings activity picks up because people are planning the week ahead. For businesses linked to nights out, Friday afternoon often works better than Monday.

## Where can you see when your followers are active?

If you have a professional account (business or creator), Instagram shows you insights. In the section with follower insights there's an overview of the **most active times**: by day of the week and by hour. A minimum number of followers is needed before the overview appears, so small profiles sometimes don't see it.

There are two things to know before you trust those numbers. First, they're shown in the time zone the app uses, so check that it's Belgrade time. Second, the most active time is also the most crowded time, because that's when everyone posts. Sometimes a post an hour earlier, before the peak, does better.

## The four-week test

Insights tell you when people are online. **The test tells you when they react to your posts**, and that's what you actually want to know. The process is simple:

!slika:najbolje-vreme-za-objavu-na-instagramu-info

1. Pick two slots, for example Tuesday at 8:00 and Tuesday at 20:00.
2. For four weeks, post the same type of content in both slots. Compare a carousel with a carousel, a Reel with a Reel.
3. After 48 hours, write down the reach and the number of saves and shares.
4. After four weeks, compare the averages. A slot that does more than 20% better becomes your slot.

Why 48 hours? Because by then most of the reach has already come in, and it's still early enough to measure every post by the same yardstick. Other metrics worth tracking are described in the article on [social media analytics](@analitika-drustvenih-mreza).

## Is it different for Reels?

Partly. Reels keep being shown to people who don't follow you for longer, so the first hours carry a little less weight than with a regular post. Early reactions still help, though, so don't post a Reel while your audience is asleep. For short video ideas, see the article on [Reels ideas for local businesses](@ideje-za-reels-za-lokalni-biznis).

With Stories, timing matters more, because a story lasts 24 hours and people watch it when they open the app. A morning story catches people on their way to work, an evening one catches the people scrolling before bed.

## What if the best slot is while you're working?

That's the most common problem for small businesses. The best time to post is exactly when the shop is full. The solution is **scheduling**: you make the post when you have some peace and quiet, and it goes out on its own at the time you set. How to do this in Meta's tools and in other apps is covered in the article on [scheduling posts on Instagram and Facebook](@zakazivanje-objava-na-instagramu-i-facebooku).

Sova schedules posts in the brand's time zone, even when you change the zone or the clocks go forward or back. Once the profile has enough posts with results, it suggests slots based on your account's history, and before that, on assumptions for your type of business. You can see what the calendar with scheduled posts looks like on the [home page](/#features).

## How often should you post, now that you know when?

A good slot without a rhythm doesn't help. If you post once every two weeks at the perfect time, the result will still be weak, because people forget about you between posts. For a local business, a good start is **three posts a week** and a few stories. The details are in the article on [how often to post](@koliko-cesto-objavljivati-na-drustvenim-mrezama), and you can lay out the whole month by following the guide in the article on [building a content calendar](@plan-objava-za-drustvene-mreze).

## The most common mistakes

- Posting whenever you get round to it, with no order at all, so you have nothing to compare.
- Trusting generic tables from the internet that were made for an American audience in a different time zone.
- Changing the slot, the type of post and the topic all at once, so you don't know what made the difference.
- Deleting a post that got off to a slow start and posting it again, which usually doesn't help.

## Frequently asked questions

### What's the best time to post on Instagram in Serbia?

For most local businesses, good starting slots are 7:00 to 9:00 in the morning, 12:00 to 14:00 and 19:00 to 21:00, Belgrade time. The right time for your profile comes from the most active times in your insights and from the four-week test.

### Is the weekend a good time to post?

It depends on the business. For restaurants, cafés and nightlife, the weekend is often the best part of the week. For services for companies, weekdays work better. Check your insights to see when your followers are active.

### Should I post at the same time every day?

It doesn't have to be the same time, but regular slots help both you and your audience. When followers know that a new offer goes up every Tuesday morning, they go looking for it themselves.

### Does a scheduled post do worse than one posted by hand?

There's no reliable evidence that Instagram penalises scheduled posts made through official tools. What matters is that the post is good and that you reply to comments in the first few hours.
