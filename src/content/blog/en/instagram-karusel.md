An Instagram carousel is a post with several photos or slides, **up to 20 in a single post**, that people swipe through. It works well for step-by-step tips, before and after shots, lists and short stories, because people save and share posts like these. The rule for success is simple: the first slide has to promise something, and every slide after it has to deliver part of that promise.

:::ukratko
- A carousel can have up to 20 slides, but five to ten is enough for most topics.
- The first slide works as a headline, so it carries **a promise, a question or a number**.
- One idea per slide, large text, the same design on every slide.
- The last slide says what to do next: save it, send a message, book.
- The 4:5 format (1080 by 1350 pixels) takes up the most screen space in the feed.
:::

## Why do carousels work so well?

A carousel holds attention longer than a single photo. Every swipe is a small decision to stay, and Instagram sees that people are spending time on the post. The second reason is usefulness: people save a carousel with five steps or ten tips so they can look at it later, and saves are one of the strongest signals that content is worth something.

For a small business, a carousel has a practical advantage too. You don't need a camera or any video editing. Good text and a simple design are enough, or a few photos from your shop arranged into a story.

## Format and technical rules

- The 4:5 format, or 1080 by 1350 pixels, takes up the most space on a phone screen.
- All the slides should be the same format. If you set the first one to 4:5, the rest adjust to it.
- You can use up to 20 slides per post, with photos, video or a mix of both.
- The text on a slide should be large enough to read on a phone without zooming. If you have to zoom in, the text is too small.

## How to build a carousel, slide by slide

Most good carousels follow the same layout:

1. A title slide with a promise: “5 mistakes that make your hair colour fade faster”.
2. The problem: why it matters, in one or two sentences.
3. The solution in steps: one step or tip per slide.
4. An example: a photo, a before and after, or a short customer story.
5. A call to action: what the reader should do next.

The first slide does the same job as the first sentence of a caption: it decides whether anyone swipes on. **Without a good first slide, the others might as well not exist.** The first sentence and the caption under the carousel are covered in the article on [how to write Instagram captions](@kako-napisati-opis-za-instagram-objavu).

## How many slides?

As many as the topic needs, not one more and not one fewer. A three-step tip is three slides plus a title and a call to action, five in total. A list of ten items is twelve slides. If a slide is only there to make the carousel longer, people sense it and give up halfway.

For most small business topics, **five to ten slides** is a good measure. Twenty makes sense for photo stories, for example a whole evening at an event or every haircut from one month.

| Type | Example | Who it's for |

| Step-by-step tips | five steps to shiny hair after colouring | salons, clinics, specialists |

## Design without a designer

You don't need a professional designer to make a good carousel. You need consistency:

- one or two fonts, always the same ones,
- two or three colours from your brand,
- the same position for the headline on every slide,
- a slide number or an arrow that tells people there's more,
- enough white space, because nobody reads a cluttered slide.

Once you've made a template, **every carousel after that is just new text**.

Check the carousel on your phone before you post it, not on a computer. What looks readable on a big screen is often tiny on a phone. If you can't read a slide in two seconds, cut the text or make the letters bigger. Watch the edges as well: Instagram sometimes crops part of the image in the feed, so keep important text away from the edges. Using the same template on every carousel builds recognition, and people know it's you in the feed before they even read your name.

## Carousel or Reel?

You don't have to choose. **Reels bring in more new people** who don't follow you yet. Carousels bring more saves and closer reading from people who already follow you. A good plan has both, say one carousel and one Reel a week. ## Frequently asked questions

### How many slides can an Instagram carousel have?

Up to 20 photos or videos in one post. For most topics, five to ten is enough.

### Which format is best for a carousel?

Portrait 4:5, or 1080 by 1350 pixels. It takes up the most screen space in the feed, and all the slides should be the same format.

### Can a carousel include video?

Yes. A carousel can mix photos and videos. A video in first place grabs attention, but a title slide with a clear promise is often the safer choice.

### Is a carousel better than a Reel?

They do different jobs. Reels bring in new people, while carousels bring saves and trust among your existing followers. A combination of the two formats gives the best results.
